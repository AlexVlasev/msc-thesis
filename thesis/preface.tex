My advisor, Andrew Rechnitzer, introduced me to the idea of studying lattice walk models with interaction, where he and his colleagues have studies some problem like this with varying success. He provided me with invaluable guidance and support along the way, and the occasional nudge to finish the thesis. I, Aleksandar Vlasev, did all the research, write-up and proofreading by myself, with some good discussions with Andrew about different issues. Along the way, I have used results and/or ideas from other mathematicians, but I have made a note of each instance, to the best of my knowledge. If a proof is missing, it is left as an exercise because it is either easy or trivial.
