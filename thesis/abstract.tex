\chapter{Abstract}

\indent In this thesis we investigate the application of an operator approach to the enumeration of lattice walks in multiple dimensions, in order to automate some of the analysis of finding the generating function $Q$ for the walks. In particular, given a lattice walk model in $d$ dimensions with an arbitrary step set, some interactions and some regional constraints, we can write down a functional equation $(1-t\Gamma S)Q = q$, where $\Gamma$ is an operator involving coefficient extractions. We also use the operator approach to unify and extend the algebraic and obstinate kernel methods. Most of the thesis focuses on models with small steps confined to the an orthant and covers generalizations in the end.
