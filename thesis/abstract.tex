\chapter{Abstract}

In this thesis we investigate the application of an operator paradigm to the enumeration of lattice walks in multiple dimensions, in order to automatize some of the procedures involved in finding generating function $Q$ for the walks. In particular, given a lattice walk model in some number of dimensions, with some regional constraints and interactions, and arbitrary step set, we may write a functional equation $(1-t\Gamma S)Q = q$, where $\Gamma$ is some coefficient extraction operator. We also use the operator perspective to unify and extend the Algebraic and Obstinate kernel methods. Most of the thesis will deal with lattice walk models with small steps confined to the non-negative orthant and the end of the thesis will cover the generalizations.
